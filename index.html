ABOUT

What is SoulTale?

SoulTale is a fan-made game inspired by the popular role-playing game Undertale. Players can choose from different human SOULs from the original game, with each one offering a unique way to play. These choices give players different abilities and playstyles while exploring a new experience inspired by Undertale.

GenreFan-made game
Playable styles5 core SOULs
